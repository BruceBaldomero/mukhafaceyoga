MOKSHA

Myofascial release massage

1 hr 10 min  ·  75

Signature all-hands myofascial release treatment.

MOKSHA means “liberation” in yoga philosophy - a release from tension and restriction.

Translated through facial therapy, this treatment uses gentle, targeted pressure across the face, neck, shoulders and jaw to release tension held within the fascia (connective tissue) and restore natural flow throughout the body.

SAVASANA

Deep relaxation and lifting massage

1 hr 10 min  ·  75

SAVASANA translates to “deep rest” in yoga - a state of complete relaxation and restoration for the mind and body.

Choose Savasana if you're seeking deep relaxation and stress relief without the focused intensity of my signature Moksha Myofascial Release. Rather than working into the fascia with sustained pressure, this treatment offers a lighter, soothing experience that leaves you feeling rested, grounded, and renewed.

Inspired by this practice, this slow-paced facial ritual is designed to encourage nervous system reset through therapeutic touch and intentional stillness.

Using organic, plant-based skincare alongside facial massage and essential oils, the treatment helps calm tension, restore balance and support healthy, radiant skin. Complementary face mask included.

Add-on or standalone

ANTAR

Buccal Release

30 min  ·  40

ANTAR translates to “inner” in yoga philosophy - representing inward awareness and release from within.

This focused intra-oral (inside-the-mouth) advanced technique works deeply into the cheeks and jaw through careful, precise massage to release stored tension and restore balance to the lower face.

The treatment is performed using nitrile gloves and is limited to 30 minutes to ensure comfort and avoid overstimulation of the area.

  • Releases deep jaw & facial tension
  • Helps relieve teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Slims and sculpts the lower face
  • Supports facial symmetry & relaxation

About

More than a facial —
a therapeutic experience

MUKHA is a holistic facial therapy space rooted in intentional touch, nervous system support, and natural restoration.

The name Mukha means "face" in both Tagalog — the language of the Philippines, where founder Mona is from — and in Sanskrit, the ancient language from which yoga originates. Reflecting Mona's Filipino heritage and her passion for holistic wellness, MUKHA embodies the connection between outer expression and inner wellbeing.

Blending facial myofascial release, buccal massage, face yoga, and holistic facial rituals, each treatment is designed to release tension held within the face, jaw, and body — while supporting natural sculpting, circulation, and deep relaxation.

Inspired by yoga philosophy and slow wellness practices, MUKHA offers more than a facial. It is a therapeutic experience created to help you slow down, soften, and reconnect with yourself.

Founded by Mona, a Registered Nurse and Holistic Facial Therapist, every treatment is guided by both clinical knowledge and intuitive care — creating a safe, nurturing, and restorative space for each client.

At MUKHA, beauty and wellness are viewed as deeply interconnected — where relaxation, wellbeing, and facial health exist together in harmony.
